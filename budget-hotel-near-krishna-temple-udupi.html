Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from pilgrims visiting Krishna Temple

How far is Hotel Bhavani from Sri Krishna Temple?

Hotel Bhavani is just 600 meters (5 minutes walking distance) from Sri Krishna Temple (Krishna Math). You can walk on the main road through Maruthi Veethika directly to the temple entrance. No auto or taxi needed.

What is the check-in and checkout time?

Check-in is available from 6 AM to 11 PM. We offer 24-hour checkout — your 24-hour stay starts from your actual check-in time. So if you check in at 6 AM for morning darshan, you have until 6 AM next day. Most other hotels force a 12 PM checkout regardless.

Is this hotel suitable for pilgrims and families?

Absolutely! Hotel Bhavani has been serving pilgrims and families for over 30 years. The area is peaceful, safe, and surrounded by vegetarian restaurants and puja shops. Many of our guests are regular devotees who visit Krishna Math multiple times a year.

How do I book a room?

Booking is simple! WhatsApp us or call 0820-2526980 one day before your visit. No advance payment needed — pay at check-in. We accept Cash and UPI payments.
