FAQ

Questions from Bus Travelers

How do I walk from Udupi Bus Stand to Hotel Bhavani?

Exit the bus stand from the main gate, walk towards Maruthi Veethika / Head Post Office road. Hotel Bhavani (Shyam Complex) is just 400 meters ahead on the main road. Takes 5 minutes by foot, even with luggage.

My bus arrives at 5 AM. Can I check in early?

Our check-in starts at 6 AM. If your bus arrives at 5 AM, you may wait briefly or call us at 0820-2526980 one day before to request early check-in. We try our best to accommodate early arrivals.

Do I need to book in advance?

We recommend calling or WhatsApping us one day before, especially during festival season or weekends. Walk-ins are possible but availability is not guaranteed during peak times.

Which buses come to Udupi Bus Stand?

Udupi bus stand has KSRTC, VRL, SRS and private buses from Bangalore (8 hrs), Mangalore (1.5 hrs), Mumbai (12 hrs), Goa (6 hrs), Hubli (6 hrs), and more. Our hotel is walkable from the bus stand regardless of which service you arrive by.
