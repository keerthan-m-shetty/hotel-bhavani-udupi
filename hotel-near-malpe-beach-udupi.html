FAQ

Beach Trip Questions

How do I reach Malpe Beach from Hotel Bhavani?

By auto-rickshaw: Step out of the hotel onto the main road, autos are always available. Fare to Malpe Beach is approximately ₹100-150. Journey takes 15 minutes.
By local bus: Walk to Udupi Bus Stand (400m), take a Malpe-bound local bus. Fare is ₹15-20.

When do boats go to St. Mary's Island?

Boats to St. Mary's Island depart from Malpe Beach boat jetty between 9 AM and 5 PM (subject to weather). The ride takes ~20 minutes each way. Best to go in the morning. Boat tickets cost ₹300-500 per person. The island is open October to May (closed during monsoon).

Why not stay at a Malpe Beach hotel instead?

Beach hotels cost ₹1500-3000/night and have limited restaurant options. At Hotel Bhavani, you pay just ₹500/night, you're in the city center with dozens of restaurants, ATMs, medical shops, and transport options. The auto to beach costs only ₹150 — you still save ₹1000+ per night!

Can I visit both Krishna Temple and Malpe Beach in one trip?

Absolutely! That's the biggest advantage of staying at Hotel Bhavani. Krishna Temple is 600m walk and Malpe Beach is a 15-min auto ride. Most guests do temple in the morning and beach in the afternoon, or split across two days.
