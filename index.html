How to Reach Us

Getting to Hotel Bhavani is easy from anywhere

✈️

From Mangalore Airport

60 km • ~1.5 hours

  • Taxi/Cab: ₹1,500 - ₹2,000
  • Bus: Take airport bus to Mangalore KSRTC, then bus to Udupi (₹150-200)
🚂

From Udupi Railway Station

5 km • ~15 minutes

  • Auto-rickshaw: ₹100 - ₹150
  • Local bus: ₹15 (Bus Stand stop)
🚌

From Udupi Bus Stand

400m • 5 min walk

  • Walk towards Maruthi Veethika / Head Post Office
  • Landmark: Shyam Complex, near Diana Circle
🚗

Self-Drive / By Car

NH-66 (Coastal Highway)

  • From Bangalore: 400 km (~7 hrs via NH-75)
  • From Mangalore: 60 km (~1.5 hrs via NH-66)
  • From Goa: 300 km (~6 hrs via NH-66)

💡 Tip: Once you reach Udupi Bus Stand, our hotel is just 400m walk. Ask anyone for "Maruthi Veethika" or "Head Post Office" - everyone knows the way!
